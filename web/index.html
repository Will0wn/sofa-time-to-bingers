Sofa Time to Bingers

Bingers imports TV Time, Trakt and Refract archives, but not Sofa Time ones. This page rewrites a Sofa Time export into one of the formats Bingers does accept. Everything runs locally. Your library is never uploaded. The only requests that leave this page are id lookups to TMDB.

1 Your Sofa Time export

Drop the .zip here or click to choose a file

    2 TMDB API key

    Sofa Time and Bingers use different databases to identify shows and films. TMDB is the only free service that can translate between the two, so the converter needs your own key to look things up.

    Free to get: sign in at themoviedb.org, open Settings, then API. Either one works. Your key is saved only in this browser and is sent to nobody but TMDB.

    Already have an old TV Time export?
